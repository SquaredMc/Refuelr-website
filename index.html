Worked example

Is a cheaper station worth the drive?

A common question with a simple answer. Here's how it plays out for a typical family car.

The scenario: a 55-litre tank, a local average of 175.9p a litre, and a station selling at 169.9p that's a 2-mile detour, in a car doing about 45mpg.

The extra 2 miles uses around 0.2 litres of fuel, about 34p. The cheaper price saves £3.30 on a full tank. So the detour costs pennies while the cheaper price saves pounds, and if you fill up every week, that's over £150 a year.

Refuelr shows the saving per tank for every station near you, so you can make that call before you set off.

FAQ

Common questions.

Is Refuelr free?

Yes. Refuelr is free and shows a small banner ad. No account or subscription.

Where do the prices come from?

From the UK Government's Fuel Finder scheme, which requires fuel retailers to report their pump prices. Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0.

How up to date are the prices?

Retailers must report a price change to Fuel Finder within 30 minutes, and Refuelr refreshes its prices from Fuel Finder every few hours. Prices can change at any time, so always check the pump before you fill up.

Which fuels does Refuelr cover?

Unleaded (E10), super unleaded (E5) and diesel. Premium diesel isn't shown yet.

Does it work everywhere in the UK?

Refuelr covers forecourts across the UK that report to Fuel Finder. Use your location or search any postcode or town. If there are only a few stations nearby, it automatically searches further afield.

Do you track my location?

Refuelr uses your location only while the app is open, to find stations near you. It's rounded to about 100 metres before it's sent, and it's never saved to our database or used to build a history of where you've been. Ads in the app come from Google, which can use approximate location based on your IP address. Our Privacy Policy has the details.

Do I need an account?

No. Install it and open the map.

A price looks wrong. What should I do?

Email hello@squaredmc.com with the station name, the town, the fuel, the price you saw and when you saw it, and we'll look into it. Our support page has more on what to include.

How is the saving worked out?

Refuelr takes the average price for your fuel across all the stations in your search area, subtracts the station's price, and multiplies the difference by your tank size. If you haven't set a car size, it uses 50 litres.

Does Refuelr show EV charging?

Not at the moment.

Is Refuelr available on Android?

Not yet. Refuelr is iOS only for now.

How do I get support?

Email hello@squaredmc.com or visit our support page.

About

Built in the UK by SquaredMc.

Refuelr is made by Lawrence and Jon at SquaredMc, a small UK app studio.

Fuel prices can vary by several pence a litre within a few miles. Since February 2026, UK forecourts have had to report their prices through the government's Fuel Finder scheme, which makes that difference visible for the first time. Refuelr puts it on a map, so you can see it before you fill up.
